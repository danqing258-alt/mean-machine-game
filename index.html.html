Mean = total ÷ number of values

Example: 🍪 2, 4, 6 cookies → (2 + 4 + 6) ÷ 3 = 4 cookies.

The mean is one number that summarizes a group. It does not mean everyone has exactly that amount.

What did we learn?

Mean = add all values and divide by how many values there are.

We use the mean to summarize many numbers with one number and compare groups. But one unusual value can pull the mean up or down!
